About the Company

Krask GmbH

Krask GmbH is an Austrian company founded in 2008 in Vienna, specializing in the supply of spare parts, consumables, and industrial equipment for the mining and aggregate processing industries.

The company supports clients across Europe, Ukraine, and the Middle East with reliable solutions for crushing and quarry equipment, helping ensure uninterrupted operation of industrial facilities.

Our key areas of expertise include:

  • supply of spare parts for crushers and industrial equipment;
  • custom manufacturing of parts according to client specifications;
  • sourcing of premium-quality OEM-equivalent components through our extensive international partner network;
  • overhaul and routine repair services;
  • technical maintenance and modernization of equipment;
  • supply of protective polymers, lubricants, and industrial consumables;
  • supply of industrial electric motors, pumps, and drilling equipment.

Krask GmbH works with globally recognized manufacturers including Metso, Terex, and Kleemann, as well as brands such as Korroflex, Densit, Rocol, Chockfast, Devcon, IrathaneFutura, and Plexus.

Our core values are product quality, fast delivery, technical expertise, and a personalized approach to every client.
